Professional Journey

Every chapter became a new capability.

From business and customer-facing roles to creative technology and software products, each stage added a practical layer to the way Ravi approaches problems and opportunities.

2013 · Education & First Career

Business foundations

Ravi completed his Bachelor of Business Management (BBM) in 2013 and began his professional career in marketing. This laid the foundation for understanding customers, communication, business needs, and practical execution.

Professional Experience

Banking, medical field & real-world operations

Over more than a decade, he built practical experience across the banking sector and medical field. Working with customers, varied business environments, and operational workflows helped him understand how services function in the real world.

A Turning Point

Choosing reinvention

A health-related turning point led him to reconsider work that involved riding a bike. Following medical advice to avoid riding and consider stable, seated work, Ravi reflected on how to rebuild his professional path while continuing to support his family. He chose to keep learning and move forward in a new direction.

Creative Technology Begins

From video editing to Blender and AI creation

Video editing became his first major step into creative technology. The learning journey expanded into Blender, graphic design, AI-assisted video generation, content creation, voice generation, and music generation. He began combining tools into connected production workflows rather than learning them in isolation.

A Milestone · DCCB Bank, Srikakulam

An early award that strengthened the journey

After seeing an Instagram announcement from DCCB Bank, Srikakulam for a short-advertisement competition, Ravi created an entry by combining AI-assisted video generation, video editing, and AI-supported content creation. About a week after submission, he received the news that he had won Second Prize. The recognition gave him confidence to continue developing his creative technology skills.

✦ Second Prize · Short-Ad Competition

Creative Work Expands

Projects beyond the immediate region

Creative project opportunities began arriving from outside his immediate region, including Odisha. Ravi continued exploring Blender and AI-based production to create visual content and advertisements with stronger storytelling, efficiency, and impact. Each project became an opportunity to test a workflow and learn a new technology.

AI & Production Toolkit

Building connected creative workflows

His toolkit grew to include ElevenLabs for voice-over generation, Suno for music and song generation, and a range of AI tools. He learned to use suitable tools together for research, ideation, voice, music, visual production, and content delivery — reviewing and refining the output as part of the process.

Software & Product Development

From creative outputs to complete products

Ravi wanted to understand how complete software products are planned, engineered, tested, deployed, and maintained — not only how individual creative deliverables are made. This led him deeper into software development, web experiences, business automation, AI-enabled applications, and product thinking.
